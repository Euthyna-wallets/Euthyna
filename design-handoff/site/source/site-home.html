How it works

Every payment
stands before the law.

In Athens, the euthyna was the audit every official faced before leaving office. Here, an agent faces it before every payment: the owners write the law, a vault with no key judges the request, and only then does the network sign.

A refusal is not a dead end: any owner can turn it into a vote on exactly that transaction.

Recovery · planned

Lose a key.
Keep every wallet.

A company's wallets have no private key, so they cannot be lost. What a person can lose is their own key. EUTHYNA will recover it with two factors, after a 48 to 72 hour delay any current key can cancel, and never holds enough to recover alone.

PASSKEY

Your phone signs.

Proves it's you, shares nothing.

ZK EMAIL

A proof from your inbox.

Someone you chose signs.
