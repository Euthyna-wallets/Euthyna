How a run works

  • On the date, anyone may start it; our keeper does. The vault pays exactly that beneficiary and amount.
  • No agent and no role budget: the members' vote is the authorisation.
  • The enclave opens the sealed account and the partner pays it in its own currency.
  • A failed run goes back to its date. Removing the beneficiary cancels its orders.
