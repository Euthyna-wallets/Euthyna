Pick a role. You set its budget next, and the founders vote on it. The agent gets its own NEAR account and can only ask the vault for what its role allows.

SELECTED
Payments agent Pay suppliers Pays registered suppliers in USDC, invoice by invoice. Allows: USDC transfers to registered suppliers, within a budget. Everything else is refused.
