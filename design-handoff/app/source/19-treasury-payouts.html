A payout to Brazil, step by step

  1. Naomi asks bank_payout(#3, 8, INV-2611)
  2. The vault checks beneficiary voted, 8 ≤ 500, budget left, reference unused. It reserves the budget.
  3. The enclave opens #3, finds the Pix channel and form, asks the partner for a quote. Proposed: refuse if the fee is over the voted 3 %.
  4. NEAR MPC signs 8 EURC to a single-use deposit address.
  5. The partner pays 74.92 BRL to the Pix key. The vault settles when Base is final.
